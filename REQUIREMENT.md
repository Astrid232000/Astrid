# ZawadieAI+ Sales Platform: Requirements

## Product

Build **ZawadieAI+ Sales Qualifier & Sales Assistant**, an AI-powered platform that helps businesses qualify leads and support sales teams.

## Lead Qualification

The system must:

* Collect and analyze lead information.
* Identify needs, intent, buying signals, and objections.
* Score leads from 0–100.
* Classify leads as Hot, Warm, or Cold.
* Explain the reasoning behind the score.
* Identify missing information.
* Suggest qualification questions.
* Recommend relevant products or services.

## Sales Assistance

The system must:

* Recommend the next best sales action.
* Generate personalized sales responses.
* Prepare concise lead summaries.
* Suggest follow-up strategies.
* Store lead analysis and outcomes.

## Business Configuration

The system must support different companies and allow each company to define its own:

* Products and services
* Ideal customers
* Qualification criteria
* Scoring rules
* Sales process
* Communication style

## Human + AI

AI recommendations must be reviewable by humans. The system must clearly distinguish available facts from AI analysis and recommendations.

## Commercial Readiness

The architecture must support future multi-company accounts, user roles, integrations, analytics, subscriptions, and scalable deployment.

## Quality

The product must be secure, responsive, reliable, easy to use, and must never invent information.
